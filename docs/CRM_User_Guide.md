# Okasha Smart CRM - User Guide

A short, plain guide for the sales team and managers. It covers the day to day: logging in, adding a
lead, logging calls and follow-ups, making a quotation, turning it into an order, handling a return,
and reading the reports.

A quick note on what you can see: the CRM only shows you your own customers. A manager sees their whole
team. You never see another agent's leads or another team's data. Prices are in rupees. Cost and profit
figures are shown only to managers.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the admin web address your manager gave you.
2. Enter your username and password and click Sign In.
3. You land on your first page. For a salesperson that is usually My Day or Leads.

If you forget your password, ask your manager or the admin to reset it. The system does not email
passwords.

---

## 2. My Day (start here each morning)

Open CRM then My Day. It shows two lists:

- Overdue: follow-ups you missed. Clear these first.
- Due today: follow-ups planned for today.

For each customer you can:

- Log call: records that you called and moves the next follow-up two days ahead. Use this for a quick
  touch when you do not need to open the full lead.
- Open: opens the full lead so you can change the stage, write a detailed note, or make a quotation.

---

## 3. Leads

A lead is a customer who is interested. Open CRM then Leads to see your list.

### Add a lead

1. Click New lead.
2. Type the customer name (required) and phone.
3. As you type the phone, the form checks for an existing customer. If a yellow box appears, that phone
   is already in the system. Click the name to open the existing lead instead of making a duplicate. If
   it really is a different person, carry on.
4. Fill in city, area, channel (how they reached you), priority, estimated value, and any notes.
5. Click Create lead.

### Work a lead through its stages

Open a lead. The stage shows where the customer is: New, Contacted, Qualified, Quotation, Negotiation,
then Won or Lost (or On hold). Use the stage buttons to move it forward as things progress. You cannot
skip stages. If you mark a lead Lost or On hold, the system asks for a reason.

### Log a call or a visit

On the lead, add an activity. Pick the type (call, WhatsApp, visit, note), write what happened, and set
the next follow-up date. Everything you log shows on the lead's timeline, newest first.

### Follow-ups

The next follow-up date drives My Day. Always set it after you talk to a customer, so the lead comes
back to you at the right time instead of being forgotten.

### Find a customer

Use the search box on the Leads page to search by name, phone, or lead code. To see everything about one
customer in one place, use Customer 360 (section 7).

---

## 4. Quotations

When a customer is ready for a price:

1. From the lead (or from the Quotations page) create a quotation.
2. Add the items, with quantity and price, and any discount. The totals add up for you.
3. Download PDF gives you a clean Okasha Smart quotation to send.
4. Share on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with a short message and the quotation number already filled in.
5. When you send it, mark the quotation Sent. The linked lead moves to the Quotation stage on its own.

If the customer wants changes, use New version. The quotation keeps the same number with v1, v2 and so
on, and only one version is active at a time. Duplicate makes a fresh quotation for a different customer
from the same items.

---

## 5. Orders

When the customer confirms:

1. If you have an accepted quotation, open it and click Convert to Order. The items carry over.
2. Fill in the delivery details: courier, tracking or CN number, payment type (COD, advance, partial)
   and amount received, and the delivery address.
3. Move the order along as it happens: Confirmed, Packed, Dispatched, Delivered. When you confirm an
   order, the linked lead is marked Won automatically.
4. Invoice PDF and Delivery slip give you printable documents in the Okasha Smart style.
5. If a delivery fails and you send it out again, use Re-attempt. The Reattempts page lists every order
   that has been tried more than once.

---

## 6. Returns

If an order comes back:

1. Open the order and click Create return, then give a reason.
2. Move the return along: Reported, then Received back, then Restocked or Damaged.

Returns feed the Returns report so the team can see why parcels come back and which courier is at fault.

---

## 7. Customer 360

Open CRM then Customer 360 and type a phone number. You get one screen with that customer's leads,
quotations, orders and returns, their total spent, and a single timeline of everything. Use it before
you call someone back so you know their full history.

---

## 8. Reports

Open CRM then Reports. Pick a report on the left and set the filters (dates, company, outlet, agent,
channel, city) at the top, then Apply. Every report only covers what you are allowed to see.

- Dashboard: your key numbers at a glance.
- Agent performance: leads, won, value and conversion per agent (managers).
- Lead funnel: how many leads sit at each stage and where they drop off.
- Pipeline: the value of open leads, weighted by how likely they are to close.
- Sales by: product, category, channel, city or outlet. You can switch between delivered orders and
  won leads.
- Lost reasons: why leads were lost.
- Returns: return rate, RTO value, and a breakdown by reason, courier, city and month.
- Lead aging and follow-up: how old your open leads are and who has overdue follow-ups.
- Repeat customers: customers who bought more than once.
- Daily summary: leads, won, delivered and revenue per day.
- Margin: revenue, cost and profit (managers only).

Export Excel and Export PDF download the current report.

---

## 9. Everyday tips

- Set the next follow-up every time you talk to a customer. That is what makes My Day work.
- Check the phone warning before creating a lead so you do not make duplicates.
- Move stages honestly. The funnel and the reports are only as good as the stages you set.
- Use Customer 360 before a callback so you have the full picture.

Questions or something not working: tell your manager or the admin.
